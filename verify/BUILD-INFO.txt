  THE TDS STATUTE TRANSITION. Rates and thresholds did not change on 1 April 2026, but
  the law did: the Income-tax Act, 2025 governs TDS on the EARLIER of the date of credit
  and the date of payment falling on or after that date, where resident TDS is a table
  inside s.393, salary is s.392 and TCS is s.394 -- and quoting the old 194-series label
  on a return is a validation failure. src/munshi/tdsStatute.ts routes on that date, and
  computeTds now returns the Act, the section to quote, the table item, the payment code
  and the cross-reference to the other Act's label alongside the rate. The references
  carry a confidence level and are not all equal: "asserted" where independent published
  mappings agree, "reported" where a code is provisional or the sources disagree (194R's
  sub-codes), "unmapped" where this ruleset carries no table item at all (194-IA, 194M) --
  and in that last case the engine says so instead of inventing a reference. An undated
  deduction still computes the rate and refuses to name a statute. The ruleset stamp moves
  to IN-2026.09 to mark the change.
